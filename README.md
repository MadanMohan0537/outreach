# Startup founder outreach

A step-by-step playbook, also usable as an AI-agent skill, for cold-emailing early-stage startup founders. It is an unofficial summary condensed from Backdoor's public posts and guides, with credit and source links throughout. Last refreshed: 2026-11-17.

## The method

1. Pick a short list of relevant startups in a domain the person already knows. Ten relevant startups beat 500 generic ones. Favor a fresh signal: raised money or grew this year, a recent hiring post, or a founder post about a problem the person has solved. Niche roles with a specific fit reply best. If visa sponsorship is needed, prioritize startups that have publicly said they sponsor, and record the source. November is a strong month. Skip any founder you can't write a real, specific reaction to.
2. Email the founder, not HR or a careers inbox. Use only a published address (their site, X or LinkedIn bio, company team or contact page, talks, GitHub, or a public founder list on Reddit or X) and record where it was found. Never guess or infer a private address.
3. Write the first email in about 15 minutes. Keep it under 100 words and phone-readable. Open with the founder's recent post, launch, or a concrete product issue, plus one real reaction. Include one thing the person built, one number, and a link. A small proof-of-work idea (a mockup, a fix, or a quick analysis) strengthens it. Ask exactly one yes/no question. Make the subject specific and about the founder.
4. Follow up once, on day 4, in the same thread, and add something new. Never "just checking in."
5. On a yes, accept, say what you'd tackle first, and ask start date, compensation, and who you'd work with. On a no, thank them and ask for one intro to another founder on the same problem, with a two-line forwardable blurb. On a rude reply, close it and move on.
6. Send a few every day. Track the signal and its source, the founder, the email and its source, the proof-work idea, the sent date, the day-4 follow-up, and the status. Drafts only: never send without an explicit go-ahead.

## Using the skill

Copy `SKILL.md` into a skills folder for Cursor or another agent (for example `.cursor/skills/startup-founder-outreach/SKILL.md`). The agent can then follow the playbook to pick targets, find founder emails, suggest proof work, and draft emails for you to review and send. The skill drafts only; it does not send mail or invent proof, metrics, or email addresses.

For the full templates and source citations, see `notes.md`. When a source post disagrees with `SKILL.md`, follow `SKILL.md`.
